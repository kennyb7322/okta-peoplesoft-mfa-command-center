-- 1. Users holding privileged permission lists (review every 6 months)
WITH PRIV AS (
  SELECT 'ALLPAGES' AS CLASSID FROM DUAL   -- example: replace with yours
  UNION ALL SELECT '<SECURITY_ADMIN_PERMLIST>' FROM DUAL
)
SELECT DISTINCT RU.ROLEUSER AS OPRID, O.OPRDEFNDESC, RC.ROLENAME,
       RC.CLASSID, O.ACCTLOCK, O.LASTSIGNONDTTM
  FROM PSROLEUSER RU
  JOIN PSROLECLASS RC ON RC.ROLENAME = RU.ROLENAME
  JOIN PSOPRDEFN  O  ON O.OPRID     = RU.ROLEUSER
  JOIN PRIV       P  ON P.CLASSID   = RC.CLASSID
 ORDER BY RC.CLASSID, RU.ROLEUSER;

-- 2. Active accounts with no sign-on in 90 days (dormant)
SELECT OPRID, OPRDEFNDESC, LASTSIGNONDTTM
  FROM PSOPRDEFN
 WHERE ACCTLOCK = 0
   AND (LASTSIGNONDTTM IS NULL OR LASTSIGNONDTTM < SYSDATE - 90);
Configuration

Access, exceptions & standards

Each control traced to the standard it satisfies and the design element that implements it.

ControlStandardImplemented by

Exception process

Requested through the service desk with a business reason and a compensating control; approved by the information security officer; time-boxed to 30, 60, or 90 days; enforced by membership in an Okta exception group that expires automatically; reported monthly.

Account recovery

Self-service recovery uses only approved factors. Service desk resets require identity verification first, are performed with a least-privilege help-desk role, and are logged. Privileged users need a stronger verification step.

Emergency access

Two vaulted accounts with dual control for Okta or gateway outages. Use alerts the SIEM immediately; accounts disable automatically after 24 hours and are reviewed after every use.
